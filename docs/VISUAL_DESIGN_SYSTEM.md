# VISUAL DESIGN SYSTEM

## Phase 10.1 — Roles & Hierarchies

This system defines the structural roles of typography, color, and surface. It deliberately avoids dictating hex codes or font families, focusing instead on the invariant hierarchies a Figma designer must fulfill.

---

### 1. Typography Hierarchy
Typography is the primary interface. It is divided strictly by cognitive role:

* **Institution Title:** The highest-level branding. Timeless, heavy, and deeply rooted in historical gravitas.
* **Exhibit Title:** (e.g., Song Name, Writer Name). Massive, high-contrast display serif. Demands immediate attention upon page load.
* **Canonical Answer:** Bold, unambiguous, and hyper-legible. Designed for 3-second comprehension.
* **Editorial Narrative:** The core reading experience. A contemporary humanist sans or highly readable serif with generous line-height, optimized for 30-minute reading sessions.
* **Research Annotation:** Dense, structured, geometric. Used for tags, dates, and metadata.
* **Evidence Citation:** Elegant, italicized or distinctly formatted typography that signals verifiable truth.

---

### 2. Color Hierarchy
Colors serve structural, not decorative, purposes.

* **Institution:** The deep background framing the experience. (e.g., Deep ink, parchment, slate). Projects permanence.
* **Knowledge:** High-contrast foregrounds ensuring absolute legibility.
* **Evidence:** Distinct, muted tones that separate archival trust from active reading.
* **Discovery:** Interactive accents. Subdued but clear signals indicating an actionable edge in the knowledge graph.
* **Research:** Highly specific contrast shifts denoting toggles, footnotes, and deep UI elements.

---

### 3. Surface Hierarchy
SufiPulse rejects the concept of a "flat webpage." Knowledge exists on distinct physical planes.

* **Exhibit Surface:** The foundational canvas of the page. Expansive and atmospheric.
* **Artifact Surface:** Elevated slightly above the exhibit. Represents a discrete, holdable piece of knowledge (e.g., A Song Card).
* **Archive Surface:** Visually inset or deeply grounded. Represents unchangeable primary evidence.
* **Research Surface:** Technical and structured, capable of handling dense matrices of questions or text without feeling overwhelming.
* **Constellation Surface:** A transparent, fluid layer floating above the content where interactive relationships (graph edges) are drawn.
