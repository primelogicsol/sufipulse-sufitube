# HIGH-FIDELITY SCREEN DESIGN: SINGER INTELLIGENCE

## The Entity: *Abida Parveen*

This document outlines the high-fidelity UI constraints for the Singer Intelligence Screen (`/knowledge/singers/[slug]`). The core philosophy is absolute: a Sufi singer is not a "celebrity performer." They are a **cultural transmitter**. This interface completely rejects modern discography layouts, Spotify-style artist profiles, and popularity metrics in favor of mapping their role as a living conduit of historical knowledge.

---

## 1. DESKTOP LAYOUT (4K / Large Display)
**Canvas:** Crisp `#F9F9FA` (Cool Off-White) to elegantly ground the human connection while maintaining absolute institutional rigor.

### Exhibit Layer (The Conduit Hero) — *The 3-Second Test*
* **Spacing:** 140px top padding. Deep, respectful whitespace. No massive "press photo" headers.
* **Meta Tags:** `11px` Monospace, `#555555`. 
  * `[ TRANSMITTER ] • [ SINDHI TRADITION ] • [ PUNJABI KALAM ]`
* **Title:** `140px` Display Serif, `#1A1815`.
  * *Abida Parveen*
* **Canonical Identity Artifact:**
  * Replaces the standard bio. `28px` Humanist Sans, centered.
  * *"A definitive transmitter of Sindhi, Punjabi, and Saraiki Sufi poetry, functioning as a primary modern conduit for the philosophies of Bulleh Shah, Shah Abdul Latif Bhittai, and Sachal Sarmast."*

### The Voice Legacy Layer — *The 30-Second Test*
* **Placement:** Immediately below the Canonical Identity, occupying the primary mid-fold.
* **Visual Presentation:** A dense, masonry-style gallery of premium Knowledge Artifact Cards, categorically rejecting "Top Tracks" lists.
* **Modules:**
  * **Signature Transmissions:** Highly curated cards for *Chaap Tilak*, *Tere Ishq Nachaya*.
  * **Recurring Concepts:** Cards linking to the philosophical ideas most dominant in her repertoire (e.g., *Ishq*, *Wahdat al-Wujud*).
  * **Associated Writers:** The poets whose legacies she physically carries.
  * **Traditions Carried:** Historical lineages (e.g., Kafi, Qawwali, Ghazal).

### The Transmission Network — *The 2-Minute Test*
* **Visual Presentation:** A full-bleed, deeply interactive constellation.
* **Structure:** Unlike Influence (outward impact) or Concept Orbits (neighboring ideas), the Transmission Network maps a *flow*.
  * *Origin Node:* Historical Tradition
  * *Path:* Writer → Poem/Song
  * *Central Conduit Node:* The Singer
  * *Output Nodes:* Concepts Manifested
* **Interaction:** Hovering over the singer highlights the glowing lines connecting 13th-century writers through her voice into modern philosophical concepts.

### Evidence Layer (The Record of Preservation)
* **Visual Presentation:** `#F2F2F4` full-width inset panel.
* **Structure:** Rejecting "Albums" and "Awards." This section acts as an institutional archive.
  * **Transmission Record:** Citations proving continuous vocal lineage (e.g., Gharana origin).
  * **Performance Record:** Historical, landmark events (e.g., specific Shrine performances, academic symposiums) rather than commercial tour dates.
  * **Source Record:** Audio/visual archives and institutional recordings treated as primary citations.

### Discovery Layer (The Infinite Loop) — *The 10-Minute Test*
* **Next Steps:** Seamlessly transitions the user away from the Singer and back into the broader knowledge graph.
  * *Discover the architect of her signature kalam:* [Bulleh Shah]
  * *Explore the central philosophy of her repertoire:* [Ishq]
  * *Investigate other transmitters of this tradition:* [Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan]

## 2. TABLET LAYOUT (Focused Lineage)
**Canvas:** `64px` side margins.

* **Exhibit Layer:** Title scales to `96px`. The Canonical Identity retains its commanding visual weight.
* **Transmission Network:** Condenses from a full-bleed map into an interactive, vertical "Transmission Tree," structurally displaying the flow of knowledge from the past (Writers) down through the present (Singer) into Concepts.
* **Voice Legacy Layer:** Modules adapt into horizontal swipe-carousels to maintain clean vertical pacing.

---

## 3. MOBILE LAYOUT (Transmitter-First)
**Canvas:** `24px` side margins.

* **Exhibit Layer:** 
  * Title: `56px`.
  * Canonical Identity immediately answers "Who is this?"
* **Voice Legacy Layer:** 
  * Massive, full-width touch targets. The user scrolls through the concepts, writers, and songs the singer carries as distinct visual blocks.
* **Transmission Network:** 
  * Converts into an expanding "Lineage Matrix" (e.g., `[ Transmits: Bulleh Shah ]`, `[ Expresses: Fana ]`).
* **Evidence Layer:** 
  * Collapsed into `[ View Institutional Archives ]`.

---

## 4. CRITICAL DESIGN RULES ENFORCED
* **The Ban List Implemented:** Total prohibition on Discography Templates, Top Tracks, Vanity Metrics (Spotify streams/Instagram followers), and Celebrity Profile imagery.
* **Transmitter Philosophy:** The interface structurally forces the user to view the Singer as an intellectual conduit linking historical writers to philosophical concepts, rather than an isolated musical celebrity.
* **Emotional Connection without Nostalgia:** The use of architectural whitespace paired with crisp, modern typography injects humanity without violating the mandate of Intellectual Clarity.
