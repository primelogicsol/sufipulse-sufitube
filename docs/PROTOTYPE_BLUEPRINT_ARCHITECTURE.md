# PROTOTYPE BLUEPRINT ARCHITECTURE

## Phase 8 — Tangible Screen Structures

This document ends abstract philosophy. Every section below defines the exact physical placement of information on a user's screen. SufiPulse is built as a Knowledge Institution; its screens must feel like physical museum exhibits optimized for the digital era.

---

## 1. Screen Blueprints

### Blueprint A: SONG INTELLIGENCE SCREEN
* **Above Fold:**
  * *Entity Hero:* Large, editorial typography displaying Song Title, Era, and Genre. Atmospheric, understated background.
  * *Canonical Answer:* A distinct, high-contrast block providing the definitive definition of the song.
  * *Primary Relationships:* Immediately visible tags linking to the primary Writer, Singer, and core Concept.
* **Mid Fold:**
  * *Meaning & Context:* Multi-column text layout detailing historical significance and deeper philosophical interpretations.
  * *Writer & Singer Variations:* Connected modules explaining authorship lineage and definitive historical performances.
  * *Concept Connections:* An organic visual constellation mapping the song to its mystical roots.
* **Lower Fold:**
  * *Evidence Archive:* Beautifully framed citation plaques linking to academic or historical primary sources.
  * *Question Intelligence:* Scannable blocks of related Canonical Questions.
  * *Research Layer:* Advanced historical footnotes hidden behind elegant toggles.
* **Discovery Exit:**
  * Curated pathways to *Related Songs*, *Related Concepts*, and *Related Writers*, ensuring infinite traversal.

### Blueprint B: WRITER INTELLIGENCE SCREEN
* **Hero:** Writer Name, Era, Title, and a definitive Canonical Summary.
* **Biography:** Long-form, highly legible narrative text.
* **Timeline:** An interactive, horizontal or vertical scrubber mapping historical movement and milestones.
* **Major Works:** A curated gallery of Knowledge Artifact Cards representing authored songs.
* **Concept Network:** A visual constellation showing the philosophical themes they championed.
* **Influence Network:** Visual edges connecting the writer to masters and disciples.
* **Evidence:** The Archive Cabinet displaying biographical primary sources.
* **Discovery:** Persistent links to adjacent Writers, Concepts, and specific Questions.

### Blueprint C: SINGER INTELLIGENCE SCREEN
* **Hero:** Singer Name, Regional/Musical Tradition, Canonical Summary.
* **Identity & Tradition:** Contextual text explaining their musical lineage and *gharana*.
* **Major Performances:** Highlighted historical performances with specific dates and venues.
* **Associated Songs:** A definitive list of their most iconic repertoire.
* **Concept Network:** The underlying mystical themes dominant in their performance style.
* **Evidence:** Archival recordings, institutional citations, and historical references.
* **Discovery:** Pathways to related Singers within the same tradition and connected Writers.

### Blueprint D: CONCEPT INTELLIGENCE SCREEN
* **Hero:** Concept Name (e.g., *Fana*) and its primary translation.
* **Definition & Meaning:** The Canonical Explanation presented in striking, isolated typography, followed by deep esoteric nuance.
* **Related Concepts:** An interactive graph linking complementary or opposing philosophies (e.g., *Baqa*).
* **Songs & Writers:** Galleries of Knowledge Artifacts showing where the concept is practically applied in poetry and music.
* **Questions:** A dense Question Bank providing immediate answers to specific philosophical inquiries.
* **Evidence:** Primary theological/philosophical citations.
* **Discovery:** Infinite loop exits to related nodes across the platform.

### Blueprint E: QUESTION INTELLIGENCE SCREEN
* **Canonical Answer:** Dominates the top of the viewport. High-contrast, unambiguous, designed to be read in under 3 seconds.
* **Context:** Supporting paragraphs expanding on the direct answer.
* **Related Entities:** Prominent visual tags mapping the actors involved in the answer.
* **Evidence:** The direct, transparent source trail proving the specific claim.
* **Related Questions:** Scannable modules providing the next logical inquiries in the research chain.
* **Discovery:** Pathways into the broader entity graph (Songs, Writers, Concepts).

## 2. Viewport Architecture

SufiPulse utilizes fluid, content-aware layouts rather than separate mobile/desktop websites.

### Desktop
* **Knowledge-Rich:** Expansive canvas.
* **Relationship-Visible:** Constellations and visual graphs live side-by-side with text.
* **Multi-Column:** Content flows asymmetrically, mimicking premium editorial layouts (e.g., Apple Books on iPad, National Geographic).

### Tablet
* **Focused:** Content dominates the central column.
* **Balanced:** Relationships and constellations gracefully stack or scale to remain interactive without overwhelming the reading experience.

### Mobile
* **Answer-First:** The Canonical Answer occupies the immediate top viewport.
* **Relationship-Second:** Tags and constellation links follow immediately beneath the answer.
* **Evidence-Third:** Sources remain accessible but are moved further down the scroll to prioritize immediate understanding.

## 3. Interaction Model

### Passive (Absorption)
* **Read:** Hyper-legible typography optimized for deep focus.
* **Scroll:** Smooth, frictionless vertical movement.
* **Explore:** Hover states on desktop reveal subtle metadata without requiring clicks.

### Active (Engagement)
* **Expand:** Toggling deep research layers or extending source citations.
* **Traverse:** Dragging or interacting with Relationship Constellations.
* **Compare:** Viewing distinct historical versions of a song side-by-side.
* **Investigate:** Opening specific evidence plaques.

### Discovery (Propulsion)
* **Follow Relationship:** Clicking a graph edge to pivot entities.
* **Open Question:** Transitioning from an entity to a specific Question Intelligence Page.
* **Open Source:** Drilling down into a primary reference.
* **Continue Journey:** Hitting the Discovery Exit to enter the next phase of the infinite loop.

---

## 4. Prototype Success Test

Before frontend implementation begins, the interactive wireframes must pass this strict psychological test when presented to an unfamiliar user:
1. **Within 10 seconds:** *"This is knowledge."* (Not a blog, not marketing).
2. **Within 30 seconds:** *"This is trustworthy."* (Evidence is visible and elegant).
3. **Within 5 minutes:** *"I want to keep exploring."* (The infinite discovery loop has engaged them).
