# VISUAL INFORMATION BLUEPRINT

## Phase 7 — The Architecture of Information Density

SufiPulse must present profound knowledge depth without inducing cognitive overload. The platform resolves this tension by deploying strict, progressive information density bands. It rejects the "religious website" aesthetic in favor of a **Cultural Knowledge Institution / Research Observatory** aesthetic.

---

## 1. Information Density Model (The 5 Bands)

Every Intelligence Page operates across five distinct bands of progressive disclosure.

### Band 1: Immediate Understanding (0-3 Seconds)
* **Goal:** Absolute clarity and canonical orientation.
* **Visibility:** Immediately visible without scrolling.
* **Content:** Entity Name, Canonical Answer, Primary Identity, and Primary Relationship.
* **Format:** Bold, sparse, high-contrast. No walls of text.

### Band 2: Context Layer (3-15 Seconds)
* **Goal:** Nuance and historical setting.
* **Visibility:** Revealed upon initial scroll or interaction.
* **Content:** Deeper Meaning, Importance, Historical Context, Primary Concepts.

### Band 3: Relationship Layer (15-60 Seconds)
* **Goal:** Launching the discovery journey.
* **Visibility:** The core interactive zone.
* **Content:** Connected Writers, Singers, Concepts, Songs, and Canonical Questions presented as Interactive Constellations.

### Band 4: Evidence Layer (As Needed)
* **Goal:** Establishing absolute trust.
* **Visibility:** Always accessible, never dominant; available instantly when verification is desired.
* **Content:** Primary sources, references, academic verification trails, and institutional provenance.

### Band 5: Research Layer (Deep Exploration)
* **Goal:** Exhaustive academic utility.
* **Visibility:** Accessible for deep users (e.g., specific toggles or sub-sections).
* **Content:** Full Question Banks, dense relationship matrices, historical footnotes, and advanced primary sources.

---

## 2. Entity-Specific Blueprints

The progressive bands dictate the precise vertical flow of information for every entity type.

### Song Intelligence Page
1. **Hero:** Title, Era, Genre, Primary Identity.
2. **Canonical Meaning:** Direct philosophical and historical definition.
3. **Lyrics / Poetry:** Original text alongside verified translation and contextual notes.
4. **Relationships:** Authorship, Singers, Performance Lineages, embedded Concepts.
5. **Evidence:** Provenance of the manuscript or traditional attribution.
6. **Research:** Deep historical questions and academic disputes.

### Writer Intelligence Page
1. **Identity:** Name, Era, Title, Core Canonical Summary.
2. **Timeline:** Historical placement and geographical movement.
3. **Works:** Associated Songs, treatises, and poetry.
4. **Concepts:** Philosophical frameworks developed or championed.
5. **Influence Network:** Masters, disciples, and artistic successors.
6. **Evidence:** Academic biographies and primary texts.

### Singer Intelligence Page
1. **Identity:** Name, Era, Primary Canonical Summary.
2. **Tradition:** Musical lineage, *gharana*, or regional association.
3. **Repertoire:** Definitive recordings and iconic adaptations.
4. **Performances:** Key historical or cultural events.
5. **Relationships:** Connected writers and overlapping concepts.
6. **Evidence:** Recorded history, institutional archives.

### Concept Intelligence Page
1. **Definition:** Canonical semantic meaning.
2. **Deeper Meaning:** Philosophical and esoteric interpretations.
3. **Relationships:** Adjacent or contrasting concepts (e.g., *Baqa*).
4. **Associated Works:** Poetry and Songs expressing the concept.
5. **Question Bank:** Comprehensive academic inquiry.
6. **Evidence:** Theological or philosophical texts.

### Question Intelligence Page
1. **Answer:** The definitive Canonical Answer (Position-Zero optimized).
2. **Context:** Expanded explanation surrounding the inquiry.
3. **Entities:** The focal actors or objects of the question.
4. **Sources:** Direct verification trail for the specific claim.
5. **Related Questions:** Paths to deeper related inquiries.

## 3. Knowledge Artifact System

The interface will be constructed entirely from five structural building blocks, rather than generic web components.

* **Artifact Type A: Canonical Answer Artifact**
  * *Purpose:* Delivering absolute truth.
  * *Form:* High-contrast, editorial typography, prioritizing immediate legibility over visual flair.
* **Artifact Type B: Relationship Artifact**
  * *Purpose:* Mapping the graph.
  * *Form:* Fluid, organic modules that visually connect nodes without resembling a corporate dashboard.
* **Artifact Type C: Evidence Artifact**
  * *Purpose:* Grounding claims in reality.
  * *Form:* Elegant, physical-feeling "archive plaques" that display citations beautifully.
* **Artifact Type D: Question Artifact**
  * *Purpose:* Capturing curiosity.
  * *Form:* Scannable inquiry blocks that invite interaction and lead to canonical answers.
* **Artifact Type E: Discovery Artifact**
  * *Purpose:* Sustaining the infinite loop.
  * *Form:* Contextual signposts acting as intelligent "Next Steps" through the knowledge network.

---

## 4. UI Priority & Institutional Rule

**Information Priority Rule:**
When screen space is constrained (e.g., Mobile), the hierarchy remains rigid:
1. Answer
2. Relationship
3. Evidence
4. Research
*(Decoration, marketing, and standard navigation are systematically deprioritized).*

**The Institutional Rule:**
The aesthetic rejects the signaling of a "religious website." It embraces the visual seriousness of a **Cultural Knowledge Institution**. The user must inherently feel: *"This is where I come to understand,"* rather than *"This is where information is stored."* Every screen radiates institutional authority and academic care.
