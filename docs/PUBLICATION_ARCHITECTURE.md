# PUBLICATION ARCHITECTURE

## Phase 6.0 — The Publication Layer

SufiPulse transitions from a private graph to a public knowledge utility through precisely five structured page types. Each type is designed to function not as a standard webpage, but as a rich, interactive "Intelligence Node."

---

## 1. Primary Page Types

### Type 1: Song Intelligence Page (`/knowledge/songs/[slug]`)
* **Hero:** Title, Era, Genre, and striking visual metaphor.
* **Canonical Answer:** The direct, authority-grade summary of the song.
* **Lyrics & Meaning:** Linguistic breakdown and mystical interpretation.
* **Writer & Singer Versions:** Historical authorship and performance lineage.
* **Concepts:** Mystical themes embedded in the composition.
* **Relationship Explorer:** Visual graph mapping to other entities.
* **Sources:** Direct academic verification.
* **Related Questions:** Intelligent pathways for further inquiry.

### Type 2: Writer Intelligence Page (`/knowledge/writers/[slug]`)
* **Biography & Timeline:** Life journey and historical context.
* **Associated Songs:** Major compositions authored.
* **Concepts:** Foundational philosophies championed by the writer.
* **Influence Network:** Masters, successors, and artistic legacy.
* **Questions:** Curated canonical inquiries.
* **Sources:** Direct academic verification.

### Type 3: Singer Intelligence Page (`/knowledge/singers/[slug]`)
* **Biography & Traditions:** Regional and musical lineage.
* **Repertoire:** Definitive recordings and performative adaptations.
* **Concepts & Writers:** Thematic focal points of their career.
* **Questions:** Curated canonical inquiries.
* **Sources:** Direct academic verification.

### Type 4: Concept Intelligence Page (`/knowledge/concepts/[slug]`)
* **Definition:** The canonical explanation.
* **Related Concepts:** Semantic map of adjacent philosophies (e.g., *Fana* ↔ *Baqa*).
* **Songs & Writers:** The practical and poetic application of the concept.
* **Question Bank:** Comprehensive exploration.
* **Sources:** Direct academic verification.

### Type 5: Question Intelligence Page (`/knowledge/questions/[slug]`)
* **Canonical Answer:** Optimized for Position-Zero AI retrieval.
* **Source Trail:** Transparent evidence chain.
* **Related Entities:** The focal points of the answer.
* **Related Questions:** Deepening the specific inquiry.

---

## 2. Experience Zones

To prevent the platform from feeling like a rigid database, every Intelligence Page is structurally divided into three distinct psychological zones:

1. **Exhibit Zone (Top Section):** Premium, immersive, and entity-focused. This is the visual and emotional hook. It presents the most critical, canonical information with museum-grade aesthetics.
2. **Intelligence Zone (Middle Section):** Dynamic exploration. It maps the relationships, canonical questions, and hidden connections. This is where users "explore knowledge" rather than just read it.
3. **Evidence Zone (Bottom Section):** Grounding and trust. Exhaustive source lists, verification trails, and reference materials confirming the authority of the page.
