# SUFIPULSE AUTHORITY DOCTRINE

## Entity: `inst_001` (The Constitutional Root Node)
This document serves as the absolute root of the SufiPulse Unified Knowledge Graph. Every constitutional entity mathematically inherits its governance rules from this doctrine.

---

## 1. Mission
SufiPulse exists to capture global search demand related to Sufism and convert that demand into rigorous, verifiable authority. It is not an encyclopedia; it is a global SEO capture engine driven by a deep, highly-verified relational knowledge graph designed to preserve the exact historical, philosophical, and performative reality of the Sufi tradition.

## 2. Scope
SufiPulse strictly documents and maps the following Authority Classes:
* `Persons` (Founders, Transmitters, Poets, Scholars)
* `Concepts` (Philosophical frameworks, Theological tenets)
* `Works` (Books, Treatises, Poetry collections)
* `Practices` (Sama, Zikr, Muraqaba, Kashf)
* `Orders` (Silsilas, Tariqas)
* `Places` (Dargahs, Khanaqahs, Historical centers)
* `Institutions` (Schools of thought, Semantic movements)
* `Events` (Historical meetings, Foundational moments)
* `Questions` (AI-targeted inquiries)
* `Evidence` (Primary manuscripts, Academic records, Oral transmissions)

## 3. Scope Exclusions (The Neutrality Firewall)
To preserve academic authority and prevent sectarian fragmentation, SufiPulse **does not** adjudicate or document:
* Political disputes (modern or historical).
* Sectarian superiority or validity claims.
* Fatwa issuance or jurisprudential rulings.
* Takfir (excommunication) narratives.
* Modern partisan conflicts.

## 4. Authority Methodology
Authority is never assumed; it is algorithmically and institutionally constructed through:
* **Evidence:** Every claim must map to the Evidence Registry.
* **Verification:** Institutional accountability via the Verification Registry.
* **Citation Depth:** Deep traceability from claim to canonical source.
* **Transmission Integrity:** Emphasizing intellectual/spiritual transmission over mere biographical trivia.
* **Confidence Assessment:** Strict stratifications (`Verified`, `High Confidence`, `Disputed`, etc.).

## 5. Dispute Policy
History contains contradictions. The platform policy is: **Document. Compare. Explain. Preserve. Never Erase.**
The Canonical Authority Layer explicitly houses conflicting claims (Claim A vs Claim B) alongside SufiPulse's canonical position and the methodological reasoning supporting it.
