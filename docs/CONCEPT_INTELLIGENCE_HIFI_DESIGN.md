# HIGH-FIDELITY SCREEN DESIGN: CONCEPT INTELLIGENCE

## The Entity: *Fana (Annihilation of the Self)*

This document defines the high-fidelity UI constraints for the Concept Intelligence Screen (`/knowledge/concepts/[slug]`). Because concepts are inherently abstract, this screen carries the heaviest burden of establishing SufiPulse as a genuine knowledge institution. It must visually manifest the concept into concrete reality across 3, 30, 120, and 600-second reading phases.

---

## 1. DESKTOP LAYOUT (4K / Large Display)
**Canvas:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) or `#F9F9FA` (Cool Off-White) background. The architecture relies on structural minimalism to maintain strict Intellectual Clarity rather than defaulting to dramatic dark-modes that risk feeling 'mystical'.

### Exhibit Layer (What is this concept?) — *The 3-Second Test*
* **Spacing:** 120px top padding. Immersive, structurally precise header.
* **Title:** `120px` Display Serif, `#0A0A0A` (Near Black).
  * *Fana*
* **The Canonical Definition:**
  * `32px` Humanist Sans, centered, max-width `900px`.
  * *"The mystical state of complete annihilation of the ego, where the individual self dissolves entirely into the presence of the Divine."*

### Intelligence Layer (Why is it important? & How does it connect?) — *The 30s & 2m Tests*
* **Grid Layout:** 50/50 Split.
* **Left Column (Thematic Expansion):**
  * Long-form editorial text detailing the theological origins and poetic evolution of the concept.
* **Right Column (Concept Orbit):**
  * A fundamentally new visual artifact. Unlike the linear Relationship Constellation, the Concept Orbit is a sweeping, circular data visualization.
  * *Center Node:* Fana
  * *Orbit 1 (Direct Counterparts):* Baqa (Subsistence)
  * *Orbit 2 (Prerequisites):* Ishq (Divine Love), Zikr (Remembrance)
  * Hovering any orbiting node rotates it to the center, seamlessly updating the contextual metadata.

### The Manifestations Layer (Where does it appear?) — *The 10-Minute Test*
* **Purpose:** To prove that *Fana* is not just an idea, but a lived tradition.
* **Visual Presentation:** A horizontally scrolling, heavily curated gallery of Knowledge Artifacts divided into distinct rows:
  * **Songs expressing Fana:** `[Chaap Tilak]` `[Tere Ishq Nachaya]`
  * **Writers discussing Fana:** `[Bulleh Shah]` `[Rumi]`
  * **Traditions emphasizing Fana:** `[Qadiri]` `[Chishti]`
  * **Performances of Fana:** `[Abida Parveen at Coke Studio]`
* *The user literally watches the abstract concept solidify into historical artifacts.*

### Evidence Layer (The Archive Panel)
* **Visual Presentation:** The background reverts to Research White (`#FAFAFA`).
* **Source Plaques:** Dense, academic citations pointing directly to foundational theological texts (e.g., Al-Ghazali, Ibn Arabi).

### Discovery Layer (The Infinite Loop)
* **Question Bank:**
  * Scannable grid of related Canonical Questions.
  * *What is the difference between Fana and Baqa?*
  * *How does Fana appear in Punjabi Sufi poetry?*

---

## 2. TABLET LAYOUT (Balanced & Immersive)
**Canvas:** `48px` side margins.

* **Exhibit Layer:** Title scales to `96px`. The Canonical Definition retains massive scale to ensure 3-second comprehension.
* **Concept Orbit:** Shifts from a persistent right-hand column into an interactive, full-width "Planetarium View" dividing the Canonical Definition from the Manifestations Layer.
* **Manifestations Layer:** Horizontal carousels adapt to show 2.5 cards simultaneously, encouraging horizontal swipe.

---

## 3. MOBILE LAYOUT (Definition-First)
**Canvas:** `24px` side margins.

* **Exhibit Layer:** 
  * Title: `64px`.
  * Canonical Definition fills the initial viewport.
* **Concept Orbit:** 
  * Complex circular visualizations fail on mobile. The Orbit transforms into a structured "Conceptual Taxonomy" list.
  * `[ Contrasts with: Baqa ]`
  * `[ Driven by: Ishq ]`
* **Manifestations Layer:** 
  * Converts into a dense, vertical accordion. Tapping "Songs expressing Fana" slides open the related artifacts seamlessly inline.
* **Evidence Layer:** 
  * Collapsed into `[ View Theological Sources ]`.

---

## 4. CRITICAL DESIGN RULES ENFORCED
* **The "Concrete Abstract" Rule:** Never allow the page to become a wall of philosophical text. The Concept Manifestations Layer must dominate the mid-page, forcing the abstract idea to anchor itself in tangible historical songs and people.
* **The Concept Orbit Rule:** Avoid hierarchical folder trees. Concepts in Sufism overlap. The circular Orbit artifact correctly visualizes the fluid boundaries between ideas like *Fana* and *Ishq*.
* **Intellectual Monumentality:** The use of stark white space, rigid structural artifacts, and precise typography separates Concepts from subjective interpretation, visually communicating that the user has entered a definitive, institutional stratum of the database.
