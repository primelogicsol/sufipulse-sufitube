# EXPANSION QUEUE

## Phase 5C — Controlled Execution Plan

The following queue dictates the strict order of operations for knowledge expansion. It prioritizes topic completeness over geographic breadth to maximize ROI for search engines and AI systems.

### Priority 1: High Authority Density Expansion (Deepen Existing)
* **Strategy:** Expand the footprint of entities already anchored in the graph. Adding depth to Rumi or Bulleh Shah generates far higher immediate authority than adding 10 obscure writers.
* **Targets:**
  * Increase Questions from 1,126 → 5,000 (Targeting ~3,800 new Q&A pairs around existing entities).
  * Increase Sources from 100 → 250 (Targeting ~150 new verified primary/academic sources).
* **Actions:** Add additional works (songs), additional associated concepts, and exhaustive Q&A coverage for the current 134 songs and 50 writers/singers.

### Priority 2: Missing Canonical Entities (Core Replenishment)
* **Strategy:** Address glaring omissions in the historical core before venturing to the geographic fringes.
* **Estimated Entities:**
  * ~50 Major Writers (e.g., Ibn Arabi, Attar, Sanai, Baba Farid).
  * ~50 Major Singers/Ensembles (e.g., Sabri Brothers extensions, classical Qawwali lineages).
  * ~200 Foundational Songs.
  * ~50 Core Concepts (e.g., Wilayah, Tariqa, Haqiqa).
* **Validation Check:** Every single new entity must meet the 3-Source, 5-Relationship, 10-Question threshold before publication.

### Priority 3: Geographic Gap Repair (Fringe Augmentation)
* **Strategy:** With the canonical core stabilized and dense, systematically fill the regions identified as "CRITICAL GAPS" in Phase 5A.
* **Estimated Entities:**
  * ~50 Writers/Singers covering West Africa (e.g., Mouride tradition, Amadou Bamba).
  * ~50 Writers/Singers covering North Africa (e.g., Shadhili masters, Al-Shushtari).
  * ~50 Entities covering the Balkans and Turkey (Bektashi, Alevi deyişleri).
  * ~20 Entities covering Western Sufi Movements and English language translations.
* **Validation Check:** Will demand meticulous primary sourcing to ensure these less universally documented traditions meet the stringent Authority Density standards.
