# EXPERIENCE FLOW BLUEPRINT

## Phase 6.7 — The Architecture of Discovery

SufiPulse transitions the traditional Web 2.0 paradigm (Information Retrieval) into a modern, graph-native paradigm (Knowledge Discovery). Users do not navigate a hierarchy; they traverse an intelligent network.

---

## 1. Entry Flows

### Flow 1: Question Entry (The Dominant Path)
Optimized for zero-click search environments and AI referrals.
1. **Entry:** Google Search → "Who wrote Chaap Tilak?"
2. **Confidence:** Lands on **Question Intelligence Page**. Immediate, authoritative Canonical Answer.
3. **Context:** User clicks the primary entity tag (*Amir Khusrau*).
4. **Deepening:** Transitions to **Writer Page**. Discovers his core philosophy (*Ishq*).
5. **Expansion:** Clicks through to **Concept Page**, finding related songs and iconic performances (*Abida Parveen*).

### Flow 2: Song Entry (The Gateway)
A song is never a destination; it is a gateway to the broader tradition.
1. **Entry:** Search → "Chaap Tilak"
2. **Context:** Lands on **Song Intelligence Page**. Explores lyrics and meaning.
3. **Lineage:** Learns about the Writer.
4. **Philosophy:** Discovers embedded Concepts.
5. **Discovery:** Pivots into Related Songs sharing the same Concept or Tradition.

### Flow 3: Writer Entry (The Historical Anchor)
1. **Entry:** Lands on **Writer Page** (e.g., *Rumi*).
2. **Philosophy:** Explores Major Concepts.
3. **Output:** Reviews Major Works.
4. **Inquiry:** Reads curated Canonical Questions about their life.
5. **Network:** Pivots to Related Writers and successors.

### Flow 4: Concept Entry (The Semantic Hub)
1. **Entry:** Lands on **Concept Page** (e.g., *Fana*).
2. **Understanding:** Reads Canonical Definition.
3. **Nuance:** Explores Related Concepts (*Baqa*).
4. **Application:** Views Associated Songs expressing the concept.
5. **Origin:** Discovers Associated Writers who defined it.

### Flow 5: Research Entry (The Academic Path)
1. **Entry:** Lands on a specific historical Inquiry.
2. **Evidence:** Reads Canonical Answer and bypasses basic context, diving straight into the **Evidence Zone**.
3. **Verification:** Inspects Sources.
4. **Cross-Reference:** Pivots to Related Sources.
5. **Deep Research:** Follows trails of Advanced Questions.

### Flow 6: The Curious Explorer (The Infinite Loop)
The ultimate measure of platform success.
**Song → Writer → Concept → Singer → Question → Song...**
The user remains in a continuous state of frictionless discovery without ever hitting a dead end or a traditional navigation menu.

---

## 2. Required Experience States

The platform must contextually adapt to the user's intent.

* **State A: First-Time Visitor**
  * *Need:* Orientation.
  * *Experience:* Emphasizes Layer 1 (Quick Understanding) and visual relationship maps to demonstrate platform depth immediately.
* **State B: Returning Explorer**
  * *Need:* Continuity.
  * *Experience:* Emphasizes Layer 3 (Relationship Exploration) and serendipitous discovery paths they haven't traversed.
* **State C: Research User**
  * *Need:* Evidence depth.
  * *Experience:* Emphasizes Layer 4 (Evidence) and Layer 5 (Advanced Questions), exposing primary sources and academic citations prominently.
* **State D: AI-Referred Visitor**
  * *Need:* Immediate answer confidence.
  * *Experience:* Emphasizes Layer 2 (Canonical Understanding). Validates the AI's referral by presenting irrefutable, structured authority immediately.

---

## 3. Required Future Metrics

Traditional metrics (Pageviews, Bounce Rate) fail to measure knowledge acquisition. The future platform will be evaluated on:

* **Discovery Depth:** Average number of distinct entity types visited per session.
* **Knowledge Paths Traversed:** Tracking the completion of full loops (e.g., Song → Writer → Concept).
* **Questions Per Session:** Engagement volume with the Q&A intelligence layer.
* **Evidence Interaction Rate:** Frequency of users expanding academic sources and citations.
* **Relationship Exploration Rate:** Clicks on graph edges (constellations) vs. traditional in-text links.
