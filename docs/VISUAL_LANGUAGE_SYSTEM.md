# VISUAL LANGUAGE SYSTEM

## Phase 9.5 — The Aesthetic of Institutional Intelligence

This system governs the visual execution of SufiPulse. The primary mandate is to project **Scholarship, Authority, Culture, Discovery, and Intelligence**.

### Strategic Warning
Designers are **strictly forbidden** from introducing:
* Generic mystical clichés (whirling dervish vectors, generic crescent moons).
* "Decorative spirituality" or overtly religious stock imagery.
* Fancy SaaS gradients or startup aesthetic dropshadows.
The platform is a Knowledge Institution, not a marketing site for mysticism.

---

## 1. Typography Families
Text is the primary interface. The typographic system must be museum-grade.
* **Display:** A high-contrast, elegant serif. Reserved exclusively for Entity Names and Hero titles to project historical weight and permanence.
* **Editorial:** A highly legible, contemporary serif or humanist sans-serif for long-form reading (e.g., Biographies, Context). Optimized for deep focus.
* **Research:** A structured, geometric sans-serif or monospaced font. Used strictly for metadata, dates, tags, and citations.
* **Annotation:** A distinct, italicized serif used exclusively for lyrical translations or esoteric footnotes.

## 2. Spacing System
* **Breathing Space:** Expansive margins around Hero sections and Discovery Exits. Signals premium curation.
* **Reading Space:** Narrow, optimized line lengths (65-75 characters) with 150%+ line height in the Intelligence Layer to prevent fatigue.
* **Research Space:** Tighter, denser padding for Evidence Layers and Source lists, signaling structured data.

## 3. Motion System
Motion is purposeful and knowledge-focused, never decorative.
* **Arrival:** Subtle, confident fade-ins. Elements load structurally, not playfully.
* **Expansion:** Smooth, immediate reveals when toggling Advanced Research or lyrical translations.
* **Relationship Traversal:** When clicking a node in a Constellation, the transition to the new entity page feels like a fluid camera pan through the graph, maintaining spatial continuity.
* **Evidence Reveal:** Citations slide into view with the deliberate weight of opening a physical drawer.

## 4. Surface System
We do not use standard "boxes." We use distinct structural surfaces.
* **Artifact:** (e.g., Knowledge Cards) Slight elevation, subtle borders. Feels like a physical card you can hold.
* **Panel:** (e.g., Answer Blocks) Flat, high-contrast background to isolate critical intelligence from the rest of the canvas.
* **Archive:** (e.g., Evidence Layer) Inset or visually grounded surface that feels structural and permanent.
* **Exhibit:** (The full page background) A soft, warm off-white or deep scholarly dark mode. Never stark `#FFFFFF`.
* **Constellation:** A transparent layer where connecting lines (edges) are drawn subtly, glowing slightly when hovered to indicate interactivity.
