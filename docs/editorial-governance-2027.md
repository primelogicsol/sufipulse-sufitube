# SufiPulse Editorial Governance & Operations (2027)

> **Directive:** A temporary architecture freeze is in effect. For the next 3–6 months, there will be no new core routes, no new frameworks, no major UI redesigns, and no database restructuring. The engineering mandate is exclusively focused on Bug Fixes, Performance, Accessibility, and Editorial/Content enhancements.

This document defines the strict editorial standards required to elevate SufiPulse from a standard music website into an academic-grade knowledge platform.

---

## 1. The 300 Master Entities (Tier 1 Authority Set)
Editors cannot select entities ad hoc. The Knowledge Graph must initially focus exclusively on a curated, foundational list:
*   **50** Foundational Saints
*   **50** Foundational Singers
*   **50** Foundational Poets
*   **50** Foundational Concepts
*   **25** Major Sufi Orders
*   **25** Canonical Texts
*   **25** Historically Significant Places
*   **25** Landmark Releases/Albums

Every page in this Tier 1 set must be exceptional before the graph expands further.

---

## 2. Editorial Workflow & Governance
Engineering no longer decides what gets published. All entities must pass through the following strict editorial workflow:
1.  **Draft**
2.  **Research**
3.  **Source Verification**
4.  **Knowledge Confidence Review** (Assigning Canonical, Strong, Moderate, Traditional, or Interpretive)
5.  **Technical Review** (Schema and semantic linkage check)
6.  **SEO Review**
7.  **Publication**
8.  **Periodic Review**

---

## 3. Version History & Transparency
Every entity page must include a visible **Editorial Record** block (now implemented in the UI sidebar). This block must display:
*   First Published Date
*   Last Reviewed Date
*   Knowledge Confidence Score
*   Number of Primary Sources
*   Number of Secondary Sources
*   Current Revision Version (e.g., v1.4)

---

## 4. Citation Standard
References must not evolve organically. Every entity must distinguish its sources according to the following hierarchy:
1.  **Primary historical sources**
2.  **Classical Sufi literature**
3.  **Peer-reviewed scholarship**
4.  **Modern biographies**
5.  **Audio and archival material**
6.  **Institutional publications**

---

## 5. Universal Editorial Style Guide
Every article must answer the same core questions to ensure total consistency across the platform.

**For People (Saints, Singers, Poets):**
*   Who was this person?
*   Why are they significant?
*   What was their historical context?
*   What teachings or works are they known for?
*   What influence did they have?
*   Which entities are most closely related?

**For Concepts:**
*   What does the term mean?
*   How is it understood across traditions?
*   Where does it appear in classical literature?
*   Which figures are closely associated with it?

---

## 6. Success Metrics
Do not focus strictly on vanity traffic. The health of the SufiPulse platform is measured by the strength of the knowledge network:
*   Percentage of entities with complete editorial sections
*   Average number of verified relationships per entity
*   Average Knowledge Confidence score
*   Number of primary and secondary sources cited
*   Internal link density
*   Entity completeness score
*   Percentage of pages updated within the last 12 months

---
**The Ultimate Goal:** Build the finest 300 Sufi knowledge pages available anywhere. Quality over quantity. Consistency over speed.
