# HIGH-FIDELITY SCREEN DESIGN: WRITER INTELLIGENCE

## The Entity: *Amir Khusrau*

This document outlines the high-fidelity UI constraints for the Writer Intelligence Screen. The visual language rigidly avoids historical nostalgia or "sepia" aesthetics, instead adopting the hyper-modern, clean precision of an elite Cultural Knowledge Institution (e.g., Financial Times meets NYPL).

---

## 1. DESKTOP LAYOUT (4K / Large Display)
**Canvas:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) or structural `#F9F9FA` (Cool Off-White) background. Deep architectural whitespace. No textures.

### Exhibit Layer (The Monument Hero)
* **Spacing:** 140px top padding. Absolute structural minimalism.
* **Meta Tags:** `11px` Monospace/Research font, `#555555`. 
  * `[ SCHOLAR ] • [ POET ] • [ CHISHTI ORDER ]`
* **Title:** `140px` Geometric Sans-Serif or highly refined modern Serif, `#0A0A0A` (Near Black). Tight kerning. 
  * `Amir Khusrau`
* **Canonical Impact Artifact:** 
  * Replaces the standard "Biography summary."
  * `28px` Serif, `150%` line height. 
  * *"The architectural bridge between Persian and South Asian mystical thought, Khusrau forged the structural DNA of Qawwali and codified the Chishti musical tradition."*
* **Note:** The user immediately learns *why he matters*, not simply *when he lived*.

### Legacy Layer & Influence Constellation (The Core Feature)
* **Visual Presentation:** A massive, full-width interactive data visualization cutting across the page below the hero.
* **Influence Constellation:**
  * Clean, vector-driven nodes connected by precise hairline edges. No glowing or "mystical" effects—pure data visualization.
  * *Center Node:* Khusrau
  * *Radiating Outward:* 
    * Traditions: *Qawwali*, *Tarana*
    * Regions: *Delhi Sultanate*, *Punjab*
    * Descendant Performers: *Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan*, *Sabri Brothers*
* **Legacy Text Module:**
  * Asymmetric block floating next to the constellation. Details modern relevance, contemporary performative traditions, and lasting cultural resonance.

### Intelligence Layer (The Dual-Column Deep Dive)
* **Grid Layout:** 70/30 split.
* **Left Column (Timeline & Works):**
  * **Historical Scrubber:** A vertical, interactive timeline mapping critical masterworks and biographical milestones, anchored by absolute dates.
  * **Works Gallery:** Structured, grid-aligned Knowledge Artifact Cards highlighting *Chaap Tilak*, *Man Kunto Maula*, etc.
* **Right Column (Concept Network):**
  * Sticky module exploring the exact philosophies he championed (e.g., *Ishq*, *Fana*), providing immediate links to Concept Intelligence screens.

### Evidence Layer (The Archive Cabinet)
* **Design:** `#F2F2F4` full-width inset panel. Feels like stepping into a climate-controlled research archive.
* **Plaques:** Structured citation cards featuring DOI links, primary Persian manuscript references, and modern academic biographies. High-contrast typography.

### Discovery Layer (The Infinite Loop)
* **Layout:** A grid of Question Artifacts and Adjacent Writer Nodes.
* **Question Artifacts:** 
  * *What was Khusrau's relationship with Nizamuddin Auliya?*
  * *How did Khusrau invent the Qawwali form?*

---

## 2. TABLET LAYOUT (Balanced & Focused)
**Canvas:** `64px` side margins.

* **Exhibit Layer:** Title scales to `96px`. Impact Artifact retains its large editorial weight to enforce immediate understanding.
* **Influence Constellation:** Shifts from a full-bleed radiating map into a concentrated "Influence Tree" occupying the center column. Edges are bolded for touch-friendly interaction.
* **Intelligence Layer:** 
  * Shifts to a single-column narrative flow. The Timeline transforms into horizontal scrolling cards rather than a vertical margin scrubber, preserving vertical height for deep reading.
* **Evidence Layer:** Condenses to a 2-column masonry grid. 

---

## 3. MOBILE LAYOUT (Answer & Impact First)
**Canvas:** `24px` side margins.

* **Exhibit Layer:** 
  * Title: `56px`.
  * Canonical Impact Artifact immediately below. 
* **Legacy Layer:** 
  * The Influence Constellation abstracts into an interactive **"Impact Matrix"** (a clean list of expanding accordions or pill-tags grouping his influence by Region, Song, and Tradition).
* **Intelligence Layer:** 
  * Timeline and Biography collapse into a seamless, highly readable vertical scroll. Works and Concepts are presented as horizontal swipe carousels to save vertical height.
* **Evidence Layer:** 
  * Hidden behind a sleek `View 8 Archive Citations` button that smoothly expands inline.
* **Discovery Layer:** 
  * Prominent, full-width tap targets answering: "What should I study next?"

---

## 4. CRITICAL DESIGN RULES ENFORCED
* **Zero Nostalgia:** The use of pure whites, stark contrast, and sharp vector geometry replaces any reliance on ancient parchment, fulfilling the mandate of a Future-Facing Knowledge Institution.
* **Not a Profile:** There is no "Avatar image" in the top left. The page is an intellectual monument to ideas, not a social media or directory profile.
* **Influence > Biography:** The immediate visual real estate is dedicated to the blast radius of his ideas (the Influence Constellation), forcefully answering *why he matters* before explaining his life story.
