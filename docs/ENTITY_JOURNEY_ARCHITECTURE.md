# ENTITY JOURNEY ARCHITECTURE

## Phase 6.3 — The Intelligence Flow

A core failure of traditional databases is the "dead end." The user arrives, reads, and leaves. SufiPulse replaces this with **Dynamic Entity Journeys**, ensuring that every page proactively answers the question: *What should the user discover next?*

### The Continuous Loop Principle
There are no terminal pages. Every entity inherently links to its adjacent nodes in the knowledge graph, creating an infinite, frictionless exploration path.

### Example Journey Maps

**Journey A: The Song Traversal**
1. **Start:** User searches for *Chaap Tilak* and lands on the **Song Intelligence Page**.
2. **Context:** They read the Canonical Answer, then click the attributed author in the Intelligence Zone.
3. **Transition:** They seamlessly enter the **Writer Intelligence Page** for *Amir Khusrau*.
4. **Deepening:** Exploring Khusrau's philosophy, they click on the concept of *Ishq*.
5. **Branching:** The **Concept Intelligence Page** lists other masterworks expressing *Ishq*. The user selects a related song.
6. **Performance:** From the new song, they click on a legendary rendition, arriving at the **Singer Intelligence Page** for *Abida Parveen*.
7. **Resolution:** They explore *Related Questions* about Abida Parveen's lineage, landing on a **Question Intelligence Page**.

**Journey B: The Question-First Traversal**
1. **Start:** User queries *Who wrote Dama Dam Mast Qalandar?* and lands on the **Question Intelligence Page**.
2. **Evidence:** They review the Canonical Answer and click the primary entity tag *Bulleh Shah*.
3. **Transition:** They land on the **Writer Intelligence Page**, discovering his network of influence.
4. **Discovery:** They follow an edge to the *Qadiri* tradition, launching a new thematic exploration.

### Execution Mandate
* Visual hierarchy must prioritize "Next Steps" logically.
* Relationships must be contextualized (don't just list "Abida Parveen"; list "Iconic Performer: Abida Parveen").
* The graph must be pre-rendered so that navigation feels instantaneous and fluid.
