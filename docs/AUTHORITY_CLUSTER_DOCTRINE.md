# AUTHORITY CLUSTER DOCTRINE

## Phase 2: Knowledge Civilization (Phase 20A)

Having finalized the Constitutional Core (Phase 1), SufiPulse shifts from building isolated anchor nodes to constructing massive, self-sustaining ecosystems of knowledge. 

**Core Strategic Pivot:** We no longer track "Published Entities" as a KPI. Publishing 5,000 shallow biographical pages creates a vulnerable encyclopedia. We now track **Completed Authority Clusters**. A single, extraordinarily deep cluster generates more AI indexing power and historical authority than hundreds of isolated pages.

---

## 1. Definition of an Authority Cluster

An Authority Cluster is an exhaustively interconnected sub-graph surrounding a central anchor node. An anchor node cannot be considered "Authoritative" until its surrounding cluster is populated, verified, and locked.

A Complete Authority Cluster must comprehensively map:
1. **The Core Person** (The anchor).
2. **Works & Literature** (Books, Treatises, Diwans).
3. **Performative Artifacts** (Specific poems, songs, Qawwalis).
4. **Concepts & Philosophy** (The exact theological/metaphysical frameworks they expounded).
5. **Transmission Network** (Teachers, Students, Silsila/Order).
6. **Geographic Anchors** (Places of birth, teaching, and Dargahs/shrines).
7. **Historical Events** (Crucial meetings, migrations, or courtly associations).
8. **AI Retrieval Layer** (50+ specific user-inquiry questions mapped to the cluster).
9. **Evidence & Disputes** (Primary manuscripts, academic consensus, and resolution of historical contradictions).

---

## 2. The Anti-Encyclopedia Rule

SufiPulse explicitly prohibits horizontal expansion without vertical depth.
* **Rule:** You may not seed a new Tier 1 or Tier 2 anchor node until the current Authority Cluster is verified at 100% completion.
* **Reasoning:** Our strategic advantage over standard encyclopedias (like Wikipedia) is not information storage, but the storage of **Transmission, Influence, Evidence, Questions, Disputes, and Relationships.** 

---

## 3. The Phase 20 Roadmap

### Phase 20B: The Amir Khusrau Cluster
* **Objective:** Expand the anchor node of Amir Khusrau into a complete ecosystem.
* **Required Scope:** Must deeply map his relationship with Nizamuddin Auliya, his foundational role in Qawwali (Practice), the invention of the Tarana (Concept/Practice), specific songs (*Chaap Tilak*, *Man Kunto Maula*), and his linguistic synthesis.

### Phase 20C: The Nizamuddin Auliya Cluster
* **Objective:** The spiritual engine behind Khusrau.
* **Required Scope:** Must map the Delhi Sultanate political tensions, the Chishti methodology of *Sama*, the network of his Khalifas (successors), and the concept of *Sulh-i Kul* (Peace with all).

### Phase 20D: The Chishti Transmission Network
* **Objective:** The institutionalization of the first three clusters.
* **Required Scope:** Connect Khusrau, Nizamuddin, Baba Farid, Qutbuddin Bakhtiyar Kaki, and Moinuddin Chishti into a verifiable, unbroken graph of evidence.

### Phase 20E: Question Authority Engine
* **Objective:** Generate and verify 1,000+ real-world AI and user queries explicitly mapped to the generated clusters (e.g., *"Why did Nizamuddin Auliya refuse to meet the Sultan?"*).

### Phase 20F: Evidence Expansion
* **Objective:** Massive expansion of the Evidence Registry using primary manuscripts, translations, and oral traditions to bulletproof the clusters against academic critique.
